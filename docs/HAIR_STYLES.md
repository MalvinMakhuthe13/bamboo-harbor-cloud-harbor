# Hair styles, next phase

The live mirror wears a photograph. Three spring groups move the left, centre, and right of that photograph. They are not braids, locs, or an afro. Nothing here is trained.

## What exists

- One plate per wig, seated so the face hole meets the brow.
- The length is split into three groups. A head flick reaches the left group first. The centre and right follow, overshoot, and settle.
- A hand near the hair pushes the groups. The face, and a hand in front, stay in front of the plate.
- Hats are a separate plate. The brim line sits on the forehead. A cap brim is lower in its photograph than a fedora brim.

## What the next phase would add

A hairstyle would be a list of chains. Each chain is a row of the same damped cards already used for the three groups, with a rest length and a stiffness.

| Style | Chains | What would differ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Box braids | One chain per braid, hanging from a scalp part | Longer rest length, less coupling between chains |
| Cornrows | Short chains along the scalp, then free ends | High stiffness until the end of the row |
| Locs | Fewer, thicker chains | Wider cards, slower settle |
| Afro | Short chains in a radius around the scalp | Small length, strong spring back |
| Ponytail | One gathered chain from the crown | The root follows the head. The tail lags |

The scalp roots would stay on the head. Only the free length would swing. A new photograph or a drawn group would be required for each style. The current plates would stay until those assets exist.

No weights would be trained. Constants would stay hand-set, or use the opt-in landmark log in `HAIR_MOTION_TUNING.md`.
